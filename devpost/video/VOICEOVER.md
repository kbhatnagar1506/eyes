## Sync points worth hitting

## Recording tips

- Record each line as its own take, then drop them on the timeline at the
  times above. `guide-voiceover.wav` has a computer voice in the right places;
  record over it, then mute it.
- Leave room for music: duck it about 12 dB under your voice; let it come up on
  the logo (0:00) and the end card (0:56).
- `eyes-product-demo.srt` has these lines as captions if you want them burned in.
